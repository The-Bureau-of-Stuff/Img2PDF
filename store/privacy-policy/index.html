ClickTo: PDF — Privacy policy

Last updated 6 August 2026

ClickTo collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores nothing about you. Everything it does happens on your own computer, and it works exactly the same with no internet connection at all.

What data is collected

None. ClickTo has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, and no telemetry of any kind. It does not build a profile of you and there is nothing for us to sell, share, or hand over, because nothing is ever gathered in the first place.

What the app does with your files

When you select images in File Explorer and choose Combine to PDF, ClickTo reads those image files in order to display thumbnails and to write the PDF you asked for. Specifically:

  • Your images are opened read-only. ClickTo never modifies, moves, renames, or deletes your source files. Rotating a page changes only the PDF that gets written.
  • The PDF is written only to the location you choose in the save dialog.
  • Image data is held in memory while you work and released when you close the window.
  • ClickTo writes a short-lived list of the file paths you selected to your user temporary folder so the app knows what you picked. That file is deleted as soon as it has been read.
  • The generated PDF records only the output filename, the creation date, and ClickTo: PDF as the producer. Your name, username, and the original file paths are not written into the PDF.

Network access

ClickTo makes no network connections. It has no update checker, no license check, no cloud processing, and no remote configuration. Your images are never uploaded anywhere, because the app has no mechanism to upload anything.

Settings stored on your device

If ClickTo remembers interface preferences such as sort order, thumbnail size, or output options, they are stored locally in the app's own settings storage on your computer. They contain no personal information, never leave your device, and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Children's privacy

ClickTo is a general-purpose document utility. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Third parties

ClickTo contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking components. It is distributed through the Microsoft Store; Microsoft's own handling of your Store account and download history is governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement and is outside our control.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be published at this same address with an updated date at the top. Since the app collects nothing, any future change would be to add a feature that does — and that would be announced in the release notes, not slipped in quietly.
